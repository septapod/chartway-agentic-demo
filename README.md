# Agentic AI Demo: Emergency Loan in Under 3 Minutes

A cinematic, single-page demo showing how an AI agent at a credit union could approve an emergency loan at 10:47pm on a Saturday night, when traditional banking is closed. A 16-year-old member named Brent needs $1,200 for car repairs. The agent gathers context, reasons through risk, generates options, and funds the loan in under three minutes.

## Why This Matters

Credit unions compete with fintechs that never sleep. Members expect instant service. When someone needs help at 10pm on a Saturday, "call us Monday" is not an answer. This demo shows what agentic AI makes possible: a full underwriting decision, made in real time, with the member's history and context driving every step.

## What You See

Split-screen layout designed for projection at a live presentation:

- **Left panel:** Phone mockup showing the member chat experience
- **Right panel:** AI reasoning panel showing the agent's internal logic

The demo walks through 7 acts:

1. **Crisis** - The member reaches out in a moment of need
2. **Data Gathering** - The agent pulls account history, payment patterns, and context
3. **AI Reasoning** - Real-time risk assessment and underwriting logic
4. **Options** - The agent generates tailored loan options
5. **Decision** - The member picks a plan
6. **Magic Moment** - Funds hit the account instantly
7. **Strategic Summary** - What just happened, and what it means for the credit union

## Background

Built in January 2026 as part of a consulting engagement presentation about agentic AI in financial services. Designed for projection at a live event, not as a production application.

## Tech

Pure HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. No frameworks, no build step. Deployed on Vercel.

## Keyboard Controls

- **Left arrow** - Previous act
- **Right arrow** - Next act
